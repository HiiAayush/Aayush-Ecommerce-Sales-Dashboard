# Aayush-Ecommerce-Sales-Dashboard

This project features a comprehensive sales dashboard designed to monitor and analyze e-commerce performance. It provides a high-level overview of key business metrics alongside granular insights into product categories, regional profitability, and customer payment preferences. 

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Tracked
* **Total Amount:** 438K
* **Total Profit:** 37K
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 5615
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** 121K

## Dashboard Features & Visualizations
* **Time & Location Filtering:** Interactive slicers to filter data by Quarter (Q1-Q4) and State.
* **Geographic Profitability:** A bar chart highlighting profit distributions across top states (Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi).
* **Category Breakdown:** A donut chart showing the share of quantity sold across categories (Clothing at 63%, Electronics at 21%, Furniture at 17%).
* **Payment Mode Preferences:** A breakdown of how customers prefer to pay, highlighting Cash on Delivery (COD) as the leading method at 44%, followed by UPI (21%), Debit/Credit Cards, and EMI.
* **Monthly Profit Trends:** A column chart tracking profit and loss variations across the calendar year (January - December).
* **Top Sub-Categories & Entities:** Additional bar charts detailing profitability across specific sub-categories (Printers, Bookcases, Sarees, Accessories, Tables) and key customers/entities.
